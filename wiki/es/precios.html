Precios y Premium

One-time payment, lifetime access, no subscriptions.

Precios model

Pollo Assistance uses a different pricing model from most AI platforms. Instead of charging a monthly subscription, it offers a pago único of $25 that unlocks all features permanently. No renewals, no recurring charges, no surprises.

There is also a fully functional free plan that lets you try the platform with no commitment.

Gratuito Plan

The free plan is available to all users who create an account. It includes:

  • 20 messages every 6 hours: A limit that resets automatically. When messages run out, a real-time countdown appears.
  • 2 MB storage: Space to save files in Pollo FILES.
  • Palos and Luces models: Access to the two free AI models (see Modelos de IA).
  • Chat IA: All basic chat features, including generación de imágenes, búsqueda web and voz.
  • Pollo STUDY and CODE: Basic access to the study and programming modules.

No credit card required: The free plan requires no payment information. You just need to create an account with email or Google.

The premium plan unlocks all advanced features of the platform:

  • Unlimited messages: No usage limits. Use the AI as much as you want.
  • 10 MB storage: 5 times more space for files.
  • Summum model: Access to the most powerful model, with 2000 tokens por respuesta (see Modelos de IA).
  • Full integration: The AI can search all your files, chats and saved code to give more complete responses.
  • Edit from chat: Ask the AI to edit your FILES or CODE files directly from the conversation.
  • Deep análisis de documentos: Advanced processing of PDFs and long documents.
  • Todas las funciones desbloqueadas: Acceso completo a todos los modulos y herramientas actuales y futuras.

Gift cards

Pollo Assistance also offers a gift card system to obtain premium. If someone gives you a card, you can redeem the code inside the app to activate your premium account instantly.

How to get Premium

  1. Go to pollo-assistance.web.app.
  2. Iniciar sesión to your account.
  3. Go to the Premium section from the menu.
  4. Make the pago único of $25.
  5. Your account updates automatically and you have lifetime access.

Why a pago único

Pollo Assistance believes in a fair pricing model. Monthly subscriptions add up over time and penalize loyal users. With a pago único, you know exactly what you pay and don't have to worry about cancelling anything.
