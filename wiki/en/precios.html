How credits work

Each AI message consumes a number of credits proportional to the tokens it uses (text in + text out + tools). The cost depends on the model and the tier:

  • Light response in Palos: ~500-1,500 credits.
  • Standard response in Luces: ~2,000-5,000 credits.
  • Deep response in Summum: ~6,000-20,000 credits.
  • Pollo CODE: multiplied by 3× (FREE) or 1.35× (Premium).

In practice, with 200,000 daily credits in FREE plan you have plenty for ~40-200 normal messages per day. With Premium (2M daily) the limit is hard to reach in normal use.

Live counter: The settings panel shows your remaining credits in real time, both daily and monthly. The platform notifies you when you fall below 20% of your monthly quota.

Why one-time payment instead of monthly subscription?

Most AI services charge $20-30 per month. Pollo Assistance bets the opposite: you pay $25 once and use the platform forever. We make money by:

  • Operating costs (Cloudflare, Firebase, AI providers) covered by the entry payment.
  • Revenue share from the extension marketplace (Pollo Assistance keeps 65% from FREE devs, 20% from Premium devs).
  • The educational version (AuraEON) is free for institutions but the costs are covered by donations and educational partnerships when they exist.

What's NOT included

Important: The $25 payment gives you lifetime access to Pollo Assistance, but does NOT give you unlimited AI calls. The platform has its own credit system: each AI response consumes credits, and we have daily/monthly limits to prevent abuse. In practice, normal-user limits are very generous.

How to get Premium

One click: Pollo Assistance has a "⭐ Premium" button in the top bar (next to AuraEON). Click it and a PayPal payment window opens directly — no redirects, no extra pages. After confirmation, your account becomes Premium automatically within a few seconds.

If you prefer manual payment or have questions, click the 💬 Support button (also in the top bar, only visible when logged in) and our AI assistant will help you. If the AI cannot resolve, the chat escalates to an admin.
