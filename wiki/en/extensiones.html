What is an extension?

An extension is a small Pollo Assistance plug-in written in .pol (Pollo Assistance's custom format, similar to JS but with built-in AI helpers). They can be utilities, themes, integrations with external services, mini-games, automations — basically anything.

For users

🔍

Browse the marketplace

Sort by popularity, downloads, rating. Filter by category (productivity, theme, integration, game).

⬇️

One-click install

The extension is installed in your account. No download, no admin permissions.

⚙️

Manage from settings

Activate, deactivate, configure or remove from your settings panel.

For developers: build your own

Open /code-editor.html and start coding. The editor includes:

  • File explorer with project organization.
  • Code editor with syntax highlight for .pol, .html, .js, .css, etc.
  • ☁ Save to Files: persist the project in Pollo FILES.
  • 📁 Load from Files: import a saved project to keep editing — project files appear ready to edit in seconds.
  • ▶ Run: execute the extension in a sandbox to test it.
  • 🚀 Publish: submit it to the marketplace for review.

Publishing process

  1. Develop and test your extension in /code-editor.html.
  2. Click 🚀 Publish: name, description, category, screenshots, price (or free).
  3. Quick review by the Pollo Assistance team (24–72h) to ensure it doesn't violate the rules.
  4. Once approved, your extension appears in the marketplace and starts generating revenue.

How payments work

Paid extensions use PayPal directly inside the modal — no redirects. When a user clicks "Buy" on a paid extension:

  1. A PayPal button appears inside the modal showing the price, the author's share and Pollo Assistance's share.
  2. After payment confirmation, the extension is installed automatically in the buyer's account.
  3. The author's revenue accumulates in their ledger (visible in their settings).
  4. Payouts to the author go out monthly when accumulated revenue exceeds €10.
Manual fallback: If for some reason the PayPal Smart Button cannot load (corporate firewalls, ad-blockers, etc.) the user gets a "Pay with PayPal" button that opens paypal.me/polloassistance/<amount> in a new tab and registers a pending purchase that the team confirms manually within 24h.
Tip: If you're not Premium yet but plan to publish many extensions, the $25 plan pays for itself with just a few sales (the 35% → 80% jump more than triples your share).
