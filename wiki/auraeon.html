What is AuraEON?

AuraEON is a specialized educational web application designed to improve coexistence, emotional wellbeing, and communication in secondary school classrooms (students aged 12–16). It gives teachers a suite of AI-powered tools to understand classroom dynamics, manage student relationships, and handle sensitive interpersonal situations responsibly.

Unlike Pollo Assistance (which is a general-purpose AI assistant), AuraEON is purpose-built for the school environment. It is not a standalone AI — it runs on Pollo Assistance technology, using a specialized educational AI model called AURA.

Quick summary

AuraEON = educational classroom management + emotional AI coaching, powered by Pollo Assistance under the hood. Your Pollo Assistance account works on AuraEON automatically — no new account needed.

User Roles

AuraEON supports three distinct user roles, each with different access and capabilities:

👩‍🏫

Teacher

  • Create and manage classrooms
  • Add students to classrooms
  • View and edit sociograms
  • Send private messages to students
  • Approve student join requests
  • Manage classroom history & timelines
  • Use the classroom AI chat (AURA) with full context
  • Access student definitions panel
  • Run attendance sessions
🎒

Student

  • Join a classroom via 6-character code
  • Participate in the classroom forum
  • Send messages to the teacher (optionally anonymous)
  • Use the Quick Consultation Chat (AURA)
  • View notifications from the teacher
🕴️

Guest

  • Access Quick Consultation Chat without an account (limited to 5 messages/day)
  • Send anonymous messages to a teacher if in a classroom context
  • Limited features — no classroom creation

Main Features

🎯 Emotional Education

AURA guides students through emotional reflection, helping them identify their feelings, understand others' perspectives, and choose constructive responses to difficult situations.

🤝 Conflict Prevention

The AI detects early signs of tension in conversations and helps users de-escalate before conflicts escalate. Serious situations are always escalated to the responsible adult.

📊 Interactive Sociograms

Visualize the social network of a classroom. Teachers can see friendship ties, romantic relationships, and conflicts at a glance — and AURA can automatically update the sociogram based on classroom conversations.

🏫 Classroom Management

Create classrooms, add students by name, share a 6-character join code with students, approve join requests, and organize everything from a central dashboard.

💬 Dual Chat Modes

Quick Chat — one-on-one with AURA for personal advice. Classroom Chat — teacher chats with AURA in the context of a specific classroom and its students.

📨 Private Teacher Messaging

Students can send private messages to the teacher, optionally anonymously. Teachers can reply in a dedicated messaging panel — perfect for sensitive topics students don't want to raise publicly.

📋 Classroom Forum

Each classroom has a forum where teachers can post announcements, share resources, and create a sense of community. Students can read and react to posts.

🔔 Notification System

Real-time notifications for join requests, new messages, and important classroom events. Badge count visible in the top navigation.

⭐ Premium Features

Some advanced tools require a Pollo Assistance Premium account:

  • Interactive Schema [BETA] — Drag-and-drop visual classroom layout editor where teachers can assign desk positions to students
  • Classroom History — A chronological timeline of classroom events, activities, achievements, and notes — exportable for AI analysis

AI: AURA

The AI assistant inside AuraEON is called AURA (Autonomous Understanding & Response AI). AURA is a specialized version of the Pollo Assistance Palos model (llama-3.3-70b-versatile via Groq), fine-tuned through its system prompt for the educational context.

What AURA can do

  • Identify and validate the emotions a student is expressing
  • Guide students through reflection before reacting to a situation
  • Encourage empathy and perspective-taking
  • Suggest constructive conflict-resolution options
  • Analyze classroom dynamics when given context (in Classroom Chat mode)
  • Automatically suggest sociogram updates based on conversations
  • Respond in a tone appropriate for 12–16 year olds

What AURA will NOT do

  • Intervene in cases of serious bullying, physical violence, or situations requiring professional help
  • Give medical or professional psychological advice
  • Take sides in a conflict
  • Speculate about student relationships without clear evidence

Safety escalation

If AURA detects keywords indicating serious harm (physical threats, intense fear, bullying) it immediately and clearly directs the user to speak with a responsible adult — a teacher, school counselor, or parent. AURA never tries to handle serious situations on its own.

How to interact with the sociogram

  • Shift + Click on two students to create a connection between them
  • Click on a line to cycle through the relationship types (friendship → romantic → conflict)
  • Right-click on a line to delete the connection
  • AURA auto-update: When you tell AURA about a relationship change in the chat, it can automatically update the sociogram using embedded commands — no manual editing needed

Classroom Tools

Student Definitions

Teachers can add private notes and context for each student ("definitions") — learning difficulties, family situations, personality notes — to help AURA give more personalized advice in the classroom chat. This information never leaves the teacher's account.

Attendance

A simple roll-call system lets teachers quickly mark who is present in each class session.

Classroom Code System

Each classroom has a unique 6-character alphanumeric code. Teachers share this code with their students, who enter it in AuraEON to request access. The teacher then reviews join requests and matches each real student to a name in the classroom list — ensuring no unauthorized users can join.

Interactive Schema Beta · Premium

A drag-and-drop canvas where teachers can arrange virtual desks and assign students to them. Useful for reflecting seating plans that influence social dynamics. Integrates with the Classroom History feature.

Classroom History Beta · Premium

A chronological timeline of events for the classroom, organized as interactive blocks:

  • 📝 Activity — Activities carried out in class
  • 🎯 Achievement — Goals reached by the group or individual students
  • 💪 Reinforcement Area — Skills that need more work
  • 📅 Important Event — Memorable classroom moments
  • 📌 Note/Observation — Teacher private notes

The timeline can be exported as plain text for use in an AI prompt — paste it into AuraEON's classroom chat or Pollo Assistance for deeper analysis.

Relation to Pollo Assistance

AuraEON and Pollo Assistance share the same underlying account and authentication system — your Pollo Assistance account works directly on AuraEON. There is no separate registration process.

Pollo Assistance provides: User authentication, AI technology (Palos model / AURA), premium account status, security infrastructure
AuraEON provides: Educational context, classroom management tools, sociograms, teacher/student workflows, emotional coaching UI
Shared database: Firebase Firestore (same project: pollo-assistance)

Think of AuraEON as a specialized lens on top of Pollo Assistance — it uses the same powerful AI engine, but focuses it entirely on the school environment.

Privacy & Safety

  • GDPR-conscious design: Student data is minimized and teachers own their classroom data
  • Anonymous messaging: Students can send messages to teachers without revealing their identity
  • No AI conversation storage: Quick Chat and Classroom Chat messages are not permanently stored in the database by default
  • Access control: Students can only join classrooms their teacher has authorized them for
  • Safety escalation: AURA always directs serious situations to responsible adults, never tries to handle them autonomously
  • Moderator oversight: Pollo Assistance moderators can intervene if necessary via the shared platform

Support

AuraEON includes a built-in support system designed to resolve technical and account issues quickly — both with AI and with real human moderators.

How to reach support

Once you are logged in, open your avatar menu (top-right) and choose 🛟 Support. A chat window opens in the bottom-right corner — this is the fastest way to get help.

The support chat is only for logged-in users

Guests can browse the wiki or contact support@auraeon.es by email. Login is required for the in-app chat so our moderators can verify your account and act on your behalf (reset data, upgrade premium, clear blocked devices, etc.).

How it works

  1. First line: AI assistant. A specialized support AI answers immediately — it knows the AuraEON platform, common errors, premium features, and can help you solve most issues on the spot.
  2. Escalation to a human moderator. If the AI cannot solve your problem (or you explicitly ask for a person), it generates a ticket with a summary of the conversation.
  3. Human reply. A real moderator picks up the ticket from the admin panel. You will receive the answer either:
    • Live in the chat — the robot icon swaps to a 📞 phone icon with "Writing…" and the moderator types directly into your support window.
    • By email — reply arrives to the address on your account from support@auraeon.es.
  4. Resolution. The ticket is marked resolved. You can reopen a new chat at any time.

What support can help with

🔐 Account issues

Password resets, email verification problems, blocked devices, suspicious login alerts, deleting your account.

⭐ Premium & billing

Premium not activated after paying, refunds, gift cards, receipts, upgrading from Pollo Assistance Premium.

🏫 Classroom problems

Students can't join, sociogram not saving, lost classroom data, removing a teacher/student, classroom code issues.

🤖 AI issues

AURA replies with errors, inappropriate content, slow responses, quota problems.

🐛 Bugs & UI

Broken buttons, mobile layout issues, missing data after an update — include a screenshot if possible.

🛡 Safety reports

Report abuse or inappropriate use of AuraEON by another account. Serious situations are escalated immediately to a moderator.

📧 Direct email

You can always write to support@auraeon.es. The in-app chat is preferred because it automatically attaches your account information, but email works if you cannot log in.

Get Started

  1. Create a Pollo Assistance account at pollo-assistance.web.app (free, takes 10 seconds)
  2. Go to AuraEON at auraeon.es — your Pollo Assistance credentials work automatically
  3. Select your role — Teacher or Student (you'll be asked on first login)
  4. Teachers: Create a classroom, add student names, and share the 6-character classroom code with your students
    Students: Enter the code your teacher gave you to join their classroom
  5. Start chatting with AURA — use Quick Chat for personal advice, or Classroom Chat (teachers) to analyze your group

🌱 AuraEON is free to use

The core features of AuraEON (Quick Chat, classrooms, sociograms, messaging, forum) are completely free. Some advanced tools (Interactive Schema, Classroom History) require a Pollo Assistance Premium account.
